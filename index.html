Over-by-Over Scoring Rate: Innings 1 vs Innings 2

Average runs scored per delivery for each over (1 to 20) across all matches

Overs 1 – 6 Powerplay Phase

Batters capitalize on fielding restrictions (max 2 fielders outside 30-yard circle). Scoring averages 1.15 – 1.35 runs/ball (~7.5 runs/over).

Overs 7 – 15 Middle Consolidation

Spinners and medium pacers control run flow. Strike rotation is key. Scoring stabilizes around 1.20 – 1.30 runs/ball with fewer boundary risks.

Overs 16 – 20 Death Overs Acceleration

All-out aggression and death bowling execution. Run rate surges drastically to over 1.75 – 2.05 runs/ball (11 – 13+ runs/over).
